ScienceBuddy

A research partner that gets better the more you work with it

Put your data, images and papers in one workspace and ask. It runs the scientific tools to search, compute and analyse, and records every step.

Use it free ↗

  • Bring your material

    Documents, images, tables and biological sequences go in with the question.

  • Tools, ready to run

    224 tools in 22 modules across genomics, pharmacology, bioimaging, literature search and database queries, on GPU-accelerated compute.

  • Every step on the record

    Each tool call is logged with its inputs and outputs, so any answer can be traced.

  • Learns from you

    Every follow-up, edit and approval teaches it, so the next run fits how you work.

In use at Fudan University, Shanghai Jiao Tong University, the Chinese Academy of Sciences and other research institutions.
