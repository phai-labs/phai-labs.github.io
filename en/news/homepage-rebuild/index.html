Homepage rebuilt around AI for Science

The PhAI Labs homepage was reorganized around model and data infrastructure for AI for Science: why scientific intelligence is the third scaling law, and how data and experiments train the model in one loop.

Published 4 Aug 2026

In August 2026, the PhAI Labs homepage was rebuilt around model and data infrastructure for AI for Science.

The claim it makes is that the third scaling law is scientific intelligence. Intelligence has run out of internet; the one input the frontier is short of is scientific data with a verified physical outcome attached. It cannot be scraped. It has to be produced.

The homepage describes this as a loop: data trains the model, and the model designs the next experiment. A model’s failures are the hypotheses worth testing; what comes back from the bench, including every failure, returns as reward, as a harder benchmark and as the next task.

This rebuild is the second version of the site, and the base on which the coming technical releases will land.
