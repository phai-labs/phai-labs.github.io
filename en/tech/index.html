DFM · Discovery Foundation Models

A research paradigm around Discovery Intelligence

DFM (Discovery Foundation Models) is neither a single model nor a software framework that forces every system through one pipeline. It is the research paradigm, capability definition and system organisation that PhAI Labs proposes around Discovery Intelligence: through formal definitions, process diagrams, system instances and real scientific cases, it turns “discovery” from a vague notion into a capability of model systems that can be learned, executed and evaluated.
